## 1. Overview & Discovery Scope

This document catalogs **every user-facing screen, tab view, nested route, modal dialog, and bottom sheet** in the **Minya Water & Wastewater Company Crisis, GIS, Valve & Fleet Management System (`incidents_managment`)** across **Flutter Web (Desktop/Tablet)** and **Flutter Mobile**.

Discovery was conducted by inspecting:
- **Root Route Table & Generator**: [`Routes`](file:///Users/alimegahed/incidents-management/lib/core/routing/routes.dart) and [`AppRouter.generateRoute`](file:///Users/alimegahed/incidents-management/lib/core/routing/app_router.dart#L50-L285)
- **Web/Desktop Application Shell (`IndexedStack` Tabs `0..11`)**: [`CrisisDashboard`](file:///Users/alimegahed/incidents-management/lib/core/future/home/ui/screens/home.dart#L36-L466) and [`AppDrawer`](file:///Users/alimegahed/incidents-management/lib/core/future/home/ui/widgets/app_drawer.dart#L11-L260)
- **Dashboard Nested Navigator**: [`DashboardRoutes`](file:///Users/alimegahed/incidents-management/lib/core/future/home/ui/widgets/dash_board/dash_board.dart#L15-L21) (`/` list & `/details` panel)
- **Mobile Application Shell & Bottom Navigation Bar**: [`MobileIncidentsListScreen`](file:///Users/alimegahed/incidents-management/lib/core/future/mobile/ui/screens/home/home.dart#L24-L413) and [`MobileMoreServicesScreen`](file:///Users/alimegahed/incidents-management/lib/core/future/mobile/ui/screens/more_services/more_services_screen.dart#L10-L374)
